## Why this checkpoint exists

This is a continuity repair. Shannon reports that this system-level intent has been recovered with Chief at least twice before and then lost across later Chief handoffs.

Do not reduce Free Frequency to an app factory, a list of critter personalities, or a backlog.

The intended system is a **HABITAT for reusable capabilities ("Parts")**.

The original goal was to build roughly 100 reusable Parts so those Parts could later be composed into whatever physical/software machines Shannon wanted. The original target machines were:

- Justine
- Wearable Lab
- Echo
- Cedric
- AI Business Sign

Apps/products/experiments are useful in their own right, but strategically they can also be proving grounds for Parts.

## Core model

**IDEA / NEED -> WORK -> EVIDENCE -> HARVEST -> PART / PROCESS IMPROVEMENT -> MEMORY -> REUSE / COMPOSITION**

Products are hosts/proving grounds. **Capability is the durable identity. Names are aliases.**

Example recovered in 2026-10-08 archaeology: Baby Eyes is a reusable visual-understanding capability currently hosted by Listing Agent/BuckList. Listing Agent is not the identity of the eye; it is a consumer/host.

The Parts Bin is therefore not merely a folder of source files. A useful Part record should eventually preserve canonical capability identity, historical aliases, implementations, evidence/tests, known consumers, dependencies, failure modes, supersession/twin lineage, reuse opportunities, and provenance.

## Lifecycle intent

A healthy Habitat should be able to support this lifecycle:

1. Accept an idea/problem/work order.
2. Identify existing Parts before rebuilding capability.
3. Perform bounded work.
4. Review evidence.
5. Harvest reusable capability and process improvements.
6. Preserve those in durable memory.
7. Connect Parts to products/machines that can reuse them.
8. Find old/forgotten Parts when relevant.
9. Identify redundant/superseded/unneeded Parts and retire them safely while preserving provenance/history.
10. Keep the Parts ecology usable for future machine composition.

"Destroy" does not mean erase history blindly. Current continuity doctrine requires provenance and supersession to survive so dead decisions do not resurrect and useful organs are not lost.

## Relationship to Factory

Factory is machinery for doing work. Habitat is the capability ecology around the work.

Do not let Factory/conveyor infrastructure become the product. A successful job should leave behind not only an output, but an answer to:

**What reusable capability did we prove, improve, reuse, or retire?**

## Relationship to Machines

The point of the Parts ecology is composition.

The long-term victory condition is not "100 apps exist." It is that enough proven Parts exist that machines such as Justine, Wearable Lab, Echo, Cedric, the AI Business Sign, and future machines can be assembled from known capability rather than repeatedly rebuilt from scratch.

**Parts are assets. Machines produce consequences.**

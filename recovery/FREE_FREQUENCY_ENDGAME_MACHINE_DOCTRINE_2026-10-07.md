## The endgame

Free Frequency is not trying to accumulate 100 unrelated little apps.

The plan is to build many small products, tools, experiments, and useful apps because each can prove a bounded capability in the real world. Those proven capabilities become reusable Parts. Parts combine into a small number of powerful integrated digital/physical machines.

**Small tool / experiment → evidence → proven capability → reusable Part → endgame machine.**

A little app may still be worthwhile on its own: useful, fun, revenue-producing, experimental, or free. But the strategic value is often the organ it proves for a later machine.

The shop should therefore preserve not only Projects and Ideas, but the relationships among:

**Idea → Experiment/Product → Evidence → Capability/Part → Endgame Machine.**

Do not flatten the shop into a backlog of apps.

## Factory doctrine

Factory is machinery for building products. Factory is not itself the product.

Infrastructure work is justified when it removes a demonstrated blocker or creates a reusable capability needed by real shop work. Every infrastructure repair must return toward the original product/consequence.

Anti-loop rule:

**Goal → attempt → evidence → demonstrated blocker → bounded repair → retry original goal.**

Reject conveyor worship:

**Goal → infrastructure → infrastructure for infrastructure → more infrastructure → original product forgotten.**

Parts are valuable, but Parts should eventually stand up as machines.

A machine is judged by useful consequences coming out the other end, not by the number of elegant components on the shop floor.

## Recursive shop

Recursion means real evidence is allowed to change the remaining plan.

**Execute bounded step → preserve evidence → review evidence → revise downstream plan if warranted → later worker consumes revised plan.**

It does not mean endlessly rebuilding the execution machinery.

Repairs are evidence-driven and bounded. UNKNOWN beats invented completion. No receipt = do not call it done.

## HQ's job

HQ is Free Frequency's institutional/shop memory, not a graveyard of scored ideas.

HQ should preserve:
- original Shannon idea/intent;
- richer later clarifications without erasing original history;
- projects and current state;
- evidence and receipts;
- reusable capabilities/Parts;
- lineage/relationships between tools, Parts, and endgame machines;
- historical experiments, failures, origin stories, and institutional lessons;
- current truth separately from historical truth.

Chief should be able to query HQ instead of carrying the entire shop in conversational context.

When new capabilities become available, HQ should make it possible to ask:

**Which old ideas or endgame machines are now newly feasible because of this Part?**

Historical ideas must not automatically become work orders. Ask:

**Does this still serve the shop Shannon is building today?**
